Mission & Purpose

The Intern Doctors Association (IDA 2026–2027) represents the unified voice and official administrative body for intern doctors serving at Southern Medical College & Hospital. Our committee coordinates clinical schedules, champions doctor safety, and manages humanitarian relief programs for impoverished patients across all hospital wards.
