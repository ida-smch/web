Our Core Mission

The Intern Doctors Association (IDA) of Southern Medical College & Hospital (SMCH) operates as the primary representative framework for intern physicians. We focus on streamlining hospital integration, safeguarding professional interests, and driving essential medical support initiatives.

Poor Fund Initiative

This initiative is funded by the members of Southern Medical College & Hospital.

Committed to Compassionate Care

Managed in close coordination with our Patients Welfare Secretary (Dr. Aka Devi) and Vice-president (Dr. Miskatul Islam), the IDA Poor Fund delivers critical financial backing for essential diagnostics and medications for distressed patients at Southern Medical College & Hospital.

We maintain that economic vulnerability should never serve as an obstacle to receiving essential healthcare services.
